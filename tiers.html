How it is calculated

  • A bill is converted to kWh by inverting the tier prices. Fees and future tariff increases are ignored.
  • Daily consumption = monthly kWh ÷ 30. It is split into a daytime and a night-time share.
  • Solar only: PV is sized to the daytime load, since surplus solar is not credited.
  • Solar + battery: PV covers the daytime load plus the energy needed to charge the battery. Battery rated capacity = energy needed ÷ (DoD × efficiency), rounded up to the nearest AVESTA LV combination (5.12 / 16 / 32 kWh).
  • Savings = current bill − new bill. Both bills use the same tier table. Payback = system price ÷ annual savings.
  • Backup hours are estimated at the home's average load, so peak loads such as AC will shorten them.
